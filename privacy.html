Privacy Policy for ScrollDebt

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ScrollDebt. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our Android application. We strongly believe in privacy and data minimalism. ScrollDebt operates 100% locally on your device and does not send any of your personal data to external servers.

2. Permissions and Data We Collect

To function properly and provide you with real-time feedback on your wasted time, ScrollDebt requires specific Android permissions:

Usage Data Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

  • Why we need it: We use this permission to monitor how much time you spend on applications you have explicitly selected to track.
  • How it is used: The data is used strictly locally to calculate your "Debt" (wasted time) and trigger Push notifications when you exceed your set threshold.
  • Data Sharing: None. Your app usage data never leaves your device. It is stored locally in an SQLite database and is never shared with us or any third parties.

Foreground Service Permission

  • Why we need it: We use foreground services to track your app usage continuously and reliably in the background.
  • How it is used: It ensures that you receive immediate Push notifications the second you cross your daily time limits.

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Why we need it: To deliver "Brutal Truth" alerts and warnings when you spend too much time on tracked apps.

3. Data Storage

All data collected by ScrollDebt (including your usage history, streaks, and tracked apps) is stored locally on your device's internal storage. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all your historical statistics will be permanently deleted.

4. In-App Purchases

ScrollDebt offers a "PRO" version via Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled securely by Google LLC. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card information or billing details.

5. Third-Party Services

ScrollDebt does not integrate with any third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics) or advertising networks. There are no trackers hidden in the app.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your email, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: apkmason.dev@gmail.com

This site uses local storage to remember your language preference. No tracking, no analytics.
