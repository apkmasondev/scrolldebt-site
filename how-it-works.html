The Mechanism of Shame

1. The Dashboard of Regret

The main screen functions as a digital mirror. It pulls precise metrics using the Android UsageStatsManager to show your total wasted time today. You see your daily streak and a real-time list of your "Top 5 Time Thieves" — the apps draining your life force, complete with smoothly animated progress bars.

2. The Brutal Truth Engine

We don't do motivational quotes. ScrollDebt utilizes a sophisticated Brutal Truth Engine containing over 200 localized, sarcastic, and painful psychological comments. The engine analyzes the structure of your time debt and delivers a tailored "roast" based on your dominant app and the length of your session. It remembers what you've seen to ensure maximum impact every time.

3. Life Lost Translations

Abstract hours mean nothing. The "Life Lost" screen converts your cumulative database history into tangible human experiences you missed out on. We translate your screen time into:

  • Sleep Cycles: 90-minute restorative sleep sessions lost.
  • Missed Workouts: 1.5-hour gym sessions you skipped.
  • Books Read: Average 250-page books you could have finished.
  • Movies Watched: Classic movies you could have caught up on.
  • Marathons Run: Marathons completed at an average pace.
  • New Skills: Mastering a new passion to an intermediate level.
  • Wasted Money: How much you'd earn working for minimum wage instead of scrolling.

4. Streak & Habit Building

Build a habit. The app tracks how many days in a row you have stayed under your daily time limit. The blood-red streak counter motivates you not to break the chain.

5. Precision Push Notifications

Set your daily threshold limit using a slider (15 to 300 minutes). The precise moment you cross it, the app fires a high-priority push notification containing a sarcastic "Brutal Truth" to pull you out of the doomscrolling trance.

6. Share My Shame

Wasted 50 hours this week? Generate a brutal, minimalist graphic for your Instagram or TikTok Story. Discover your dynamic rank, ranging from a green "Rookie" to a crimson "Brain Dead".
